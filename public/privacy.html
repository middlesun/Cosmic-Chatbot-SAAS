Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026 • Finola Solutions

This Privacy Policy describes how Finola Solutions ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or use the SmartPoster web dashboard and browser extension.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in (for example, via Google OAuth), we receive basic profile details such as your name, email address, and avatar URL.
  • Content & Workspace Data: Campaign profiles, templates, URLs, notes, and settings that you choose to save in the dashboard or browser extension.
  • Billing Information: Paid upgrades are processed through our payment provider (Dodo Payments). Finola Solutions does not store your full payment card details.
  • Analytics & Technical Data: Standard usage metrics such as page views, browser type, and referring URLs collected through analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to help us improve the product.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain SmartPoster features across the web app and extension.
  • To sync your saved campaigns, settings, and plan entitlements across sessions.
  • To process subscription payments, manage active plans, and prevent fraud.
  • To respond to customer support requests and improve site performance and reliability.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with trusted infrastructure and service providers strictly necessary to run SmartPoster (such as hosting, authentication/database providers, payment processing, and analytics services), or when required by law.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use cookies and browser storage (such as localStorage and extension storage) to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and measure website traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain your account and workspace data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.

6. Changes to This Policy

Finola Solutions may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please reach out to Finola Solutions via our Contact form.
